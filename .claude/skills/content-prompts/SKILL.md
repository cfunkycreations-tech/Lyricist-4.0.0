# Content prompts (still + video)

Chris makes clips in sections and stitches them. Give him this every time, no questions first.

## Format

- 8 to 10 shots, vertical 9:16, 4 to 8 seconds each.
- Every shot gets TWO prompts, labeled `Still:` then `Video:`.
  - Still = the first frame, a full scene description.
  - Video = what moves, written for image-to-video. Starts "The woman from the starting image..." and covers action, camera move, length.
- Last AI shot is the end card background (spinning vinyl, 4s). Plus one real screen-record insert of the app (AI garbles app screens). Say where to cut it in.
- Prompts talk to the AI, never to Chris. Each one is complete on its own and paste-ready. No "same rooftop", no "[lock line]", no "as before", no "Shot 2 continued".
- Say once: generate the still first, feed it to the video tool, send the mp4s and Claude stitches them with ffmpeg.
- Titles in bold, prompts in blockquotes. Captions and overlay text go in a short edit list at the end, never inside prompts.

## The woman (repeat in full in every Still)

Glamorous woman, 27, long tousled dark hair, bold red lip, silver rings, black t-shirt with the attached LYRICIST PRO badge printed large and centered on her chest, worn black leather jacket fully open and pushed back off her chest.

- Always an adult, glamorous, tasteful, non-explicit.
- Chris attaches the LYRICIST PRO badge image and his best still to every generation.
- Close every Still with: `Logo sharp, fully visible, unobstructed, text unchanged. 90s music-magazine style, warm amber light, 35mm film grain, shallow depth of field, vertical 9:16.`
- Every Video prompt ends with: `Her shirt logo stays sharp and unchanged.`
- Guitar is always held low on the hip, below the chest logo. Never in front of it. No arms or hair over the logo.
- Badge also goes on the guitar case (large sticker) or painted on the kick drum when that prop is in frame. Then: "Both logos stay sharp and unchanged."

## Default shot list

1 record store hook, 2 rooftop writing, 3 rooftop stuck then phone, 4 phone reaction, (real app insert), 5 singing at mic, 6 guitar low on hip, 7 listening back in headphones, 8 rooftop hero finish, 9 vinyl end card.

## Rules

- No on-screen text inside prompts. Overlays are added in the edit: hook text on shot 1, "Lyricist Pro, $19.99 once. Link in bio." over the real logo on the end card.
- Never use: neon, midnight, rain, heartbreak, cold coffee, shadows, whispers, echoes, sparks, cage, gravity, chains, storm.
- Never call the app free. Price is $19.99 once.
- Write like a person. "The songs in your head deserve to be finished", not "deserve a finish". No AI-sounding phrasing in captions.
- Terse. No explaining, no menus, no "want me to...".
