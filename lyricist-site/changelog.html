CHANGELOG

What changed, in plain language. Newest at the top.

4.2.0.141

August 22, 2026

  • The Quantum Lab opens straight on your keywords now, instead of a header taking up the top of the screen.
  • The lock button under Section DNA tells you whether the lattice is locked, instead of you having to guess.
  • Finished songs file themselves into Recordings automatically, even if you never press Keep.

4.2.0.135

August 22, 2026

  • A song that finishes now comes home by itself. Every take is saved the moment it lands, and nothing is lost when you switch tabs.
  • Long songs no longer choke on their way back into the app, so a full length track arrives the same as a short one.
  • A button under your takes pulls in any song already on your computer that the app has not picked up yet.

4.2.0.132

August 22, 2026

  • The description of your song now actually reaches the engine that makes the music. It never did before, which is why songs came back generic.
  • The assistant can no longer wipe your lyrics. If it replaces them, there is a button to put your words back.
  • The section tags you click are checked after the lyrics are written, so an intro or a bridge you asked for does not get quietly dropped.
