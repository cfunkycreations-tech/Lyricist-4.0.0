From blank page to finished song

Four moves. One window.

  1. Pick the sound

    Choose a genre, a mood and a voice. Or name an artist you love and Ghost Rider breaks down how they write.

  2. Write it

    Songwriter drafts verses, choruses and the bridge. Polish any line, lock what you like, redo what misses.

  3. Hear it

    Send it to Black Hole Studios and hear it sung by a full band. Or copy the lyrics and Suno tags straight into Suno.

  4. Finish it

    Master the track, add cover art, and export the album. Loop, chop, record and split stems along the way.

Black Hole Studios

Your lyrics. Sung by a full band.

Your words go in and a real song comes out, with vocals and a band behind them. Pick a genre, a mood and a voice, and it writes the song description for you. Short songs run free on the cloud engine with no key. Full-length songs run on your own free Kaggle account, connected with one button.

  • Free cloud engine
  • Genre, mood, voice
  • Every take saved
  • Shows the wait before you start

Ghost Rider

Name any artist. Write in their style.

It breaks down how they write: the flow, the imagery, the way they build a hook. Then it helps you write a brand-new song in that style, with Suno tags for that sound. It never copies their lyrics.

  • Style reports
  • Suno tags
  • Originality check

The Matrix

Build a verse from word tiles.

Drop your keywords into a 20-tile grid. Heat words up, lock the rhymes you want, and it writes the verse around them. Stuck lines come loose fast.

  • 20-tile word grid
  • Lock the rhymes
  • One-click verse

The studio

A four-track loop station, Chopped & Screwed, a MIDI studio, a stem splitter, a recording booth and a mastering suite. They all work offline with no key at all.

  • Works offline
  • No key needed
  • Exports the album

Questions
Straight answers.

    

      
Is this a subscription?
No. You pay once and keep it, updates included.

      
Does it work with Suno?
Yes. Songwriter and Ghost Rider write the lyrics and the Suno style tags to match. Copy them into Suno and generate. Black Hole Studios can also sing your song right inside the app.

      
Do I have to pay for AI?
No. The writing tools run on a free OpenRouter key, with no card. Short songs in Black Hole Studios run free on the cloud engine, and full-length songs run on a free Kaggle account.

      
Does it work on a Mac?
The full app is for Windows 10 and 11. The phone version runs on iPhone and Android, and it comes with your purchase.

      
Windows says "Windows protected your PC." Is that a virus?
No. Windows shows that box for installers that aren't signed with a paid certificate. Click More info, then Run anyway.

      
How do I get the phone version?
Open lyricist-pro.pages.dev in Safari on iPhone or Chrome on Android, add it to your home screen, and enter the license key from your receipt.
