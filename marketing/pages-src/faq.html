Suno and AI

Does it work with Suno?

Yes. Songwriter and Ghost Rider write the lyrics and the Suno style tags to match. Copy them into Suno and generate. Black Hole Studios can also sing your song right inside the app.

Do I have to pay for AI?

No. The writing tools run on a free OpenRouter key, with no card. Short songs in Black Hole Studios run free on the cloud engine, and full-length songs run on a free Kaggle account.

Is my OpenRouter key safe?

Your key goes straight from your computer to OpenRouter. Lyricist Pro never sees it.

Does Ghost Rider copy other artists' lyrics?

No. It studies how an artist writes, then helps you write a new song in that style. It never copies their lyrics, and the Suno tags it writes never name the artist.

What works with no key at all?

All of this is ready the moment it installs:

  • Black Hole Studios (short songs)
  • Recording Booth and RC-Funk 5000
  • Mastering Studio, Stemmer and MIDI Studio
  • Chopped & Screwed
  • Thesaurus, Dictionary and Scratchpad

Installing and the phone

Windows says "Windows protected your PC." Is that a virus?

No. Windows shows that box for installers that aren't signed with a paid certificate. Click More info, then Run anyway.

How do I get the phone version?

Open lyricist-pro.pages.dev in Safari on iPhone or Chrome on Android, add it to your home screen, and enter the license key from your receipt. Turn the phone sideways; the studio needs the width.

Where is my license key?

On your Gumroad receipt, and in your Gumroad library under Lyricist Pro.
