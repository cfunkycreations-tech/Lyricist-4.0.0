Get started

Up and writing in ten minutes.

You bought Lyricist Pro. Here's everything from the download to your first song, on the PC and on your phone.

  1. Run the installer

    Download the installer from your Gumroad library and open it. Windows shows a blue box that says "Windows protected your PC". That isn't a virus warning. It shows because the installer isn't signed with a paid certificate.

    More info→Run anyway

  2. Switch on the writing tools

    Get a free key at openrouter.ai. It takes about two minutes and doesn't ask for a card. Then open Settings in the app and paste it in.

    Your key goes straight from your computer to OpenRouter. Lyricist Pro never sees it.

  3. Press Play Demo

    On any tab, press Play Demo and the Ghost takes the mouse and walks you through it, out loud. Pause or skip any time. Everywhere else, hover anything for a tip.

  4. Put it on your phone

    Open lyricist-pro.pages.dev in Safari on iPhone or Chrome on Android.

    iPhoneShare→Add to Home Screen
    Android⋮ menu→Add to Home screen

    Enter the license key from your receipt once, and turn the phone sideways.

  5. Optional: full-length songs

    Short songs in Black Hole Studios run free on the cloud engine with no key. For full-length songs, make a free Kaggle account. Black Hole Studios walks you through connecting it with one button.

Stuck?

Check the FAQ, or email cfunkycreations@gmail.com. New versions show up in your Gumroad library.
