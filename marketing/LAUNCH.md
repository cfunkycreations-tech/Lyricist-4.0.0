## What to keep making (one a day for 30 days)

1. **You, 10 seconds, on camera.** You and the guitar, and why you built it. Your face and that story are the strongest thing you've got.
2. **The comment game.** Post "Give me an artist and a topic." Then answer
   comments with a video of the app making it. It never runs out of ideas.
3. **Typed words, then the song.** Show the lyrics on screen, then the song
   playing. Short, and the payoff lands in the first 3 seconds.
4. **The songs themselves.** Every song you make is a post. Put "made in
   Lyricist Pro" on screen.
5. **The price.** Big studio software versus $19.99 once.

The rules that matter: hook in the first second (text on screen, no slow
intro). Reply to every comment in the first hour. Don't delete posts that
flop. Recording a run on the PC with OBS takes about a minute. Send it to me
and I'll make the cuts.

## Free places beyond the apps

- **Reddit:** r/Songwriting and r/WeAreTheMusicMakers. Read each one's rules
  first, because most ban straight ads. Share the story and the songs, and give
  the link when people ask.
- **Facebook** songwriter and home-studio groups. Same approach.
- **Product Hunt:** one launch day, free. Use the covers from `gumroad/`.

## Paid, later

Wait until one post clearly beats the rest. Then boost that exact post (TikTok
Promote, or the YouTube equivalent) at a small daily amount, and watch Gumroad
sales before raising it. Never pay to push a video that didn't work for free.
