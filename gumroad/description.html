Words in. Songs out.

Lyricist Pro is a songwriting studio for Windows with 18 tools in one window. Write the lyrics, shape them, hear them sung by a full band, then master the album. One payment, no subscription.

Black Hole Studios

The one that surprises people. Your words go in and a real song comes out, with vocals and a full band behind them. Pick a genre, a mood and a voice, and it writes the description of the song for you. It runs free on the cloud engine with no key, and every take saves itself. Longer songs take longer, and it tells you how long before you start.

The Ghost Demo

New to a tab? Press Play Demo and the Ghost takes the mouse and walks you through it, out loud. Pause or skip any time. Everywhere else, hover anything for a tip.

Everything in the box

  • Songwriter: set the style, mood and topic, then write and polish the lyrics line by line.
  • Ghost Rider: name any artist. It breaks down how they write, then helps you write a new song in that style. It never copies their lyrics.
  • Song Forge: one idea in, a full set of lyrics and matching cover art out.
  • The Matrix: build a verse from a grid of word tiles. Heat words up, lock the rhymes, and it writes the verse.
  • Collaboration: write with someone else at the same time, wherever they are. Straight between your two computers, no account, no server holding your song.
  • RC-Funk 5000: a four-track live loop station. Stack layers, catch a riff or a vocal hook while you write.
  • Stemmer: split any song into vocals, drums, bass, guitar, keys and other, right on your own computer.
  • Recording Booth: record guitar, harmonica or vocals straight into the app and keep every take.
  • Voice Lab: write a script, pick a voice, shape it, and save it as a WAV.
  • MIDI Studio: turn any audio into editable MIDI on a full piano roll, with a visualizer running behind it.
  • Mastering Studio: EQ, compression and limiting, then export the finished album with its cover art.
  • Chopped & Screwed: slow a song until the voice sinks with it, then chop it back up on the beat. The sound DJ Screw invented in Houston.
  • Rhyme Helper, Thesaurus, Dictionary: perfect, slant and internal rhymes; the word you were reaching for; meanings in English and Spanish.
  • AI Tools Hub and Scratchpad: a shelf of AI tools worth knowing, and a notepad for the ideas that show up at the wrong time.

What you need

  • A Windows 10 or 11 computer.
  • For the writing tools: a free key from OpenRouter. It takes about two minutes and does not ask for a card. Your key goes straight from your computer to OpenRouter; Lyricist Pro never sees it.
  • Ready the moment it installs, no key at all: Black Hole Studios, Recording Booth, RC-Funk 5000, Mastering Studio, Stemmer, MIDI Studio, Chopped & Screwed, Thesaurus, Dictionary and Scratchpad.
